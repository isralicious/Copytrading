Before You Join

Questions from the family

Where is my money held? +
In your own broker account, always. Copytrading mirrors our strategy's trades into your account, we never take custody of your funds, and you can disconnect or withdraw at any time.
Why is there a waitlist and a countdown? +
The strategy performs best when the whole community launches together. We open in allocation windows so everyone starts at the same time, with enough members connected. When the timer ends and we're ready, copytrading goes live for the whole family at once.
How is revenue shared? +
On a transparent 70/30 profit split in your favour: you keep 70% and the team earns 30%, charged only on profits, so our incentive is your profit. Full terms are shared with you before you connect any capital.
What is the expected maximum drawdown? +
Drawdown is the temporary dip your account can experience. Expected maximums by tier: Low risk 20–25%, Medium risk 30–40%, High risk 50–70%. These are targets for normal conditions, not hard limits, and markets can always move beyond them. Only connect capital you can afford to lose.
What are the expected gains for each tier? +
Target gain ranges by tier: Low risk 3–6% per month, Medium risk 8–15% per month, High risk 20–35% per month. These are targets only, not guarantees or predictions. Returns vary with market conditions and losing periods are normal, and higher targets always come with higher drawdown.
Which risk tier should I choose? +
Most members start with Balanced Gold. Choose Steady if capital preservation matters most, or Apex if you're experienced and comfortable with larger swings for higher potential upside. You can change tiers later.
Do I need a specific broker? +
We support the major brokers used by our community. If you don't have an account yet, tell us in the form and we'll guide you through setup before launch.
Is profit guaranteed? +
No. FX trading carries real risk and past performance never guarantees future results. We focus on disciplined, institutional-grade risk management, but you should only connect capital you can afford to lose.
